Privacy Policy

Noir Video · last updated November 17, 2026

1. Summary

Noir Video (“the app”) is a video player for the files on your device and for network streams you open, published by TeamCoolApps.

  • Your videos, playlists, resume positions and settings are kept on your device. They are never uploaded to the developer.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. The app has no server of its own.
  • The app shows one banner ad from Google AdMob, above the navigation bar, never in the player. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), Google’s consent form is shown first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Online subtitle search is off by default and needs your own OpenSubtitles API key. If you use it, the search details listed in section 5 are sent to OpenSubtitles.
  • The app contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.

2. Data we collect

The developer collects no personal data through the app. It does not receive your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, videos, playlists or viewing history.

The Google AdMob SDK that shows the banner may collect the following when an ad is requested:

AdMob never receives your videos, stream addresses, subtitles or viewing history: the app gives the ad SDK nothing from them. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The app has no server of its own. It goes online only for the following:

Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging PlatformThe data listed in section 2, and your consent choiceWhen the banner is shown and when the consent form is needed

Third-party SDKs:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) — one anchored banner. No interstitial, rewarded or full-screen ads.
  • Google User Messaging Platform — asks for ad consent where the law requires it and stores your choice on the device.

Playback libraries bundled in the app (Media3/ExoPlayer, FFmpeg decoders, libass, libVLC) run on your device and contact only the addresses you open. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no social SDK.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is a general-purpose video player intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

The developer stores nothing about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete.

  • To delete everything the app keeps on your device: Android Settings → Apps → Noir Video → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Your video files and saved screenshots are not touched.
  • To remove the app’s Android backup: Android Settings → Google → Backup (the wording depends on your device).
  • OpenSubtitles requests are handled by OpenSubtitles under its own terms and privacy policy.

Data collected by Google AdMob is retained by Google under its own policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

8. Security

Local data is protected by Android’s app sandbox: other apps cannot read the app’s private data without root access. Subtitle searches and ad traffic are transmitted over HTTPS; a stream you open uses whatever protocol its address uses. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but the app keeps the data it sends to the minimum needed for each feature.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data behaviour changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes on Google Play. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
